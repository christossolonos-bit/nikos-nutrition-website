Confidentiality

How your details are stored, and when they are deleted.

Your personal information is stored only until we give you your results, then it is destroyed. The exception is if you choose to continue with Lamakko for closer analysis — in particular current athletes who want to maintain fitness or compete. In that case we keep your data until you no longer want to be a client.

Lamakko does not share this information with anyone outside the company, including third parties. Anyone working with Lamakko treats your data as confidential and stores it securely until it is destroyed. This follows the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
