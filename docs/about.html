About Lamakko

A small UK practice for people who want nutrition advice that stands up to training, not generic diet tips.

I’m Nick.

I advise on food, supplements and the link between nutrition and physical fitness. The people I help are usually training with a competition in mind — or trying to get good enough that one becomes realistic.

The work combines nutrition with exercise physiology: what your sessions demand, how you recover, and whether your current eating pattern can support that.

If I am not the right person, I will say so.

I reply within one working day on email, WhatsApp or phone.
